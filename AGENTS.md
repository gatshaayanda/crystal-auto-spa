# Crystal Auto Spa — project instructions

## Product and source of truth
- Product: **Crystal Auto Spa**, Mogoditshane, Botswana.
- This repository was deliberately created from `gatshaayanda/big-brothers-car-wash` because the product owner selected that working car-wash foundation. Do not switch to Vula, Iteration 13, or another repository without explicit instruction.
- The product owner is the final reviewer. Implement working software, not plans alone. The current repository, this file, the latest owner brief, actual code and Firestore rules are authoritative.
- The supplied logo direction is a white wordmark with a faceted blue crystal-drop/C mark and the subtitle **MOGODITSHANE • BOTSWANA**. Source assets: `public/crystal-auto-spa-logo.svg` and `public/crystal-auto-spa-icon.svg`.
- Do not invent the public domain, phone number, exact address, opening hours, service names, prices, capacity, availability or payment provider. Keep business settings and service catalogue editable by staff. An unconfigured price is not a zero price.

## Implementation workflow
- Inspect current branch/head and the exact files/flows before editing. Make focused changes in place; never reclone over existing work or rewrite history.
- Preserve working authentication, Firestore data contracts, CRUD, booking, queue, receipts, account ownership, notifications, chatbot and PWA behavior unless the owner explicitly authorizes a change.
- On unexpected behavior: stop, inspect the actual state and first failure, fix that failure only, then verify.
- Do not add Firebase Storage or Firebase Storage rules. The owner will configure the independent Firebase project/database. Do not hardcode or reuse another project's project ID, credentials, tokens, customer data or deployment identifiers.
- Never claim a build, test, push, device test or deployment succeeded without evidence. The owner handles Vercel unless delegated.

## Customer journeys
- The homepage's primary customer action is **Book a Wash**. Returning customers can open **My Account** for receipts, visit history and repeat booking.
- Keep the on-site walk-in intake accessible from the homepage's subdued ON-SITE TEAM utility. Do not put staff sign-in in front of the customer handoff intake.
- Walk-in intake remains concise: customer name, Botswana WhatsApp number (+267 plus eight local digits), optional vehicle description, an active staff-published priced service or a clearly labelled proposed amount, optional notes and any approved optional tip. Do not make vehicle registration mandatory.
- A customer-proposed amount is not an agreed charge. A published service price is not proof of payment. Staff records the final transaction and payment facts separately.
- Only show active staff-published services in customer selectors. Do not invent prices or show an unpriced service as a priced offer.
- A queue entry is shared only after Firestore confirms the write. Offline drafts are device-local until server-confirmed sync. Use stable IDs and idempotent retry; never claim a draft is visible to staff.
- WhatsApp is a prepared handoff, not an automated send. The customer/staff member must press Send in WhatsApp. Never claim delivery.
- Booking submissions are requests until staff confirms them. Enforce availability and capacity on the server; never promise a preferred slot as confirmed.
- Receipts distinguish walk-in tickets from completed transaction receipts. Never show a walk-in ticket as proof of a completed wash or payment. Receipt links are time-limited and scoped; account claims require verified ownership.
- Keep guest continuity, customer UID ownership, private messages and receipt access server-scoped. A typed phone number alone does not prove ownership.
- Customer photo/video submissions are optional, consent-gated and pending moderation until staff approves them. A successful upload is not a public post.

## Staff and admin operations
- The existing `/admin` workspace is the staff operations surface. Preserve its real booking, queue, services, settings, gallery, reviews, loyalty, racing and inbox workflows.
- Staff authorization is a 24-hour server-verifiable session. The client timer is only UX; every privileged API and Firestore rule must enforce authorization and expiry.
- Preserve live persistence, validation, error/retry, loading, empty and success states. Do not fake CRUD or optimistically claim a server write.
- Queue order and wait estimates are staff-entered operational values, not promises generated by the app.
- Never imply a payment, discount, notification, WhatsApp delivery or customer contact happened unless the underlying system confirms it.

## UploadThing and Firebase boundaries
- **UploadThing is the only media-upload provider.** Configure `UPLOADTHING_TOKEN` in the deployment environment. Current router limits: images 4 MB, videos 16 MB, PDFs 4 MB.
- Staff media uploads must pass the existing verified staff session. Customer ride media uploads must be tied to a valid, unexpired receipt token. Preserve consent, moderation and metadata persistence.
- Do not import `firebase/storage`, call `uploadBytes`/ `getDownloadURL`, add `storage.rules`, or deploy Firebase Storage rules.
- Firestore rules and indexes are separate and remain in `firestore.rules` and `firestore.indexes.json`. Preserve least-privilege rules; do not weaken them to bypass configuration problems.
- The owner will create/configure the independent Firebase project and provide the web config, service account, authorized domains, auth providers, Firestore rules/indexes and VAPID key. The repository must use environment variables and must not default to Vula's project.
- Keep secrets out of source control. Required environment variables are documented in `.env.example`; real values belong only in local/deployment secret stores.

## PWA installation, offline and chatbot protections
- PWA installation is protected infrastructure. Preserve early `beforeinstallprompt` capture, the retained native prompt event, explicit user-click prompt, branded dismissible card, close/reopen behavior, embedded-browser handling, iOS Safari Share → Add to Home Screen guidance, service-worker registration and update/refresh lifecycle.
- Never add a fake install prompt, automatic blocking modal, duplicate install card, alert or dead install button. Only identity, assets, copy, theme colors and this app's cache namespace may change without separate approval.
- Preserve the offline shell and honest local-draft/sync semantics. Cache public shell/assets and approved racing practice assets only. Never cache private account, admin, receipt or API data as public content.
- Preserve the inherited chatbot behavior and features. Change branding and Crystal Auto Spa-specific content only unless explicitly authorized.
- Keep racing mechanics, controls, scoring, progression and offline behavior unchanged during ordinary branding work.

## Visual and operational quality
- Inspect the actual Iteration 13 reference image at `gatshaayanda/iteration13/docs/assets/washride-home-reference.jpeg` before visual planning or QA. Transfer its visual quality, mobile-first hierarchy, compactness, readable typography, blue contrast, clear primary action and scannable shortcuts; do not copy WashRide branding.
- Apply the visual standard across homepage, booking, customer account, receipt, staff/admin, forms, lists and detail states. Homepage quality alone is not acceptance. Avoid oversized headings, bulky generic cards, excess whitespace and verbose copy; keep touch targets accessible.
- The homepage should establish the brand immediately, explain the service, and make booking obvious. Booking should be grouped and compact. Account screens should prioritize receipts/history/rebooking. Admin should be dense but easy to scan with clear section separation.
- Follow the Operational Transparency Standard: show what is loading, locally saved, uploading, server-acknowledged, pending, failed or complete. A spinner, browser online event or selected file is not proof of persistence.
- Keep interfaces mobile-first and responsive at desktop widths. Do not claim visual completion without inspecting running screenshots.
